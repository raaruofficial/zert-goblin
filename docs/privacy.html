Zert Goblin — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

"We" is Raaru, the publisher of Zert Goblin. Contact for privacy questions, data requests, and deletion requests: raaruofficial@gmail.com.

2. Scope — stated plainly

Zert Goblin is an arcade game. It runs as an app on your device talking to a game server we operate. There is no account, no email address, and no password in the product. Instead, your game state is keyed to your Solana wallet: to play, you connect a wallet and sign a short server-issued proof message (a "wallet session"). The signature proves you control that wallet; it moves no funds and reveals nothing beyond the wallet's public address. Without a connected wallet there is no persistent player state — only the public leaderboard is readable. There is no analytics or advertising service in the product, and no third-party data recipient other than the public infrastructure named in §5.

3. Data the server holds

The game server keeps per-wallet game state and its history, in a database we operate. Every row below belongs to one wallet address — there is no shared cabinet, and one wallet's state is never visible to or spendable by another:

  • Arcade state (per wallet): free plays used, paid play credits, lifetime-pass ownership, cumulative spend, the currently selected skin, and an optional .skr display label — the state that makes your plays, balance, and skins persist between sessions.
  • Wallet sessions and proof nonces: when you connect, the server stores a one-time nonce (5 minutes) and, once your wallet signs it, a session record (wallet address + a random session token, 24 hours). Sessions are how the server knows a request comes from your wallet's owner.
  • Game sessions and runs: a session token per play, tied to your wallet, and for each submitted run the player name you entered, score, coins, longest combo, Goblin Mode count, survival time, mode, and timestamp. Runs are what the public leaderboard displays (player name + run stats).
  • Skins and editions: which skins your wallet owns, and Zald God edition records (edition number, the purchasing wallet, and — if that wallet had set one at purchase time — its .skr display label; edition holders are displayed in the skin store).
  • .skr display labels: a .skr name you choose to set as a display label for your wallet, stored only after your wallet signs a server proof message for it. A label is display text only: it grants nothing. (An earlier free-skin perk tied to .skr names is paused and confers nothing in this build.)
  • Issued purchase quotes: when you open checkout, the server issues a quote bound to your wallet — product, token, exact amount, token price, a server-generated reference, and a 10-minute expiry — and records whether it was redeemed, voided, or left to expire. Quotes are how the server knows a payment is the one it priced.
  • Payment ledger (verified_payments): for every purchase, the transaction signature, product, payment token (SOL or USDC), the USD price and token amount, the quote source and time, the per-purchase reference, the payer wallet address, and the verification time. This ledger is the receipt book: it is also what makes a payment redeemable exactly once. Retention limit: 90 days after a purchase, the ledger row is tombstoned — the payer wallet and the quote-detail fields are erased, while the transaction signature (or its hash), the product, and the USD price are retained for replay defence and accounting. See §7.
  • Logs: the product stores no access/IP log table and collects no IP-address or device identifier in its code.

4. What we do not collect

  • No private keys, no seed phrases — ever. Payments are signed in your wallet; the app and server never see your keys.
  • No email addresses, passwords, phone numbers, or location data.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising identifiers. No sale of personal data to anyone, for any reason.

5. What leaves your device or the server — the complete list

  • To the Solana public RPC (https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com): payment-verification lookups carrying the transaction signature you submit, and wallet-side requests your own wallet makes. Payments are public Solana transfers to the game's treasury wallet; your wallet address, the amount and the time will be visible on-chain to anyone.
  • To CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com): server-side SOL/USD price quotes. No user data rides along — the request carries no identifier.
  • To Jupiter (only if you open "Stack real ZEC"): the swap runs in Jupiter's official plugin under its own terms; your wallet interacts with Jupiter directly. The game server receives nothing from it.
  • To the Solana Mobile Seeker Connect relay (relay.solanamobile.com) — only when you connect a wallet through Seeker Connect in a browser: your browser first opens Solana Mobile's connect page to reach your wallet, and the wallet session then runs over a relay Solana Mobile operates. The session's messages — connection and signing requests and their responses — pass through the relay encrypted between the game and your wallet, so the relay routes them without being able to read their contents. Like any relay operator, it can see the routing metadata: that a session occurred, when, its size, and the network addresses of the two endpoints. Inside the Seeker app itself, the wallet connection is local to the device and no relay is involved. The game server receives nothing from the relay, and nothing in this flow changes what the game server holds (§3).
  • To your own client: cabinet state, leaderboard, and store data.
  • That is the complete list of recipients.

6. How data is used

Only to run the game: persist plays/balances/skins per wallet, display the leaderboard, issue and redeem purchase quotes, verify payments and prevent their double-redemption, and display .skr labels their owners set. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

7. Retention and deletion

Class Retention What you can ask for
Per-wallet state, sessions, runs, skins, .skr labels Kept while the service operates Email raaruofficial@gmail.com and identify your wallet — be ready to prove it is yours by signing a short message with that wallet. We delete our rows and confirm within 30 days.
Issued quotes Redeemed/voided quotes are kept with the payment ledger; unredeemed quote records are erased on the same 90-day cycle Covered by the same request path.
Payment ledger Buyer attribution is erased automatically 90 days after purchase (payer wallet + quote-detail fields nulled; transaction signature, product, and USD price retained for replay defence and accounting). Fully attributed rows exist only inside that 90-day window. Same request path — inside the window we can delete the attributed row on request. Note the underlying Solana transaction itself is a permanent public record no deletion can reach — that is the chain's nature, not our retention.
Leaderboard runs Part of the game's public board while the service operates Covered by the same deletion request; removing a run removes it from the board.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Zert Goblin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The product collects no age information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact raaruofficial@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version at the same URL and update the effective date above.
