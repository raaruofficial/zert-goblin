1. What Zert Goblin is

Zert Goblin is a single-player arcade game: a four-lane ZEC collector with combos, Goblin Mode multipliers, a global leaderboard, and a cosmetic skin store. It is a parody work. It is not affiliated with Mert, Helius, or Zcash, and nothing in the game is endorsed by them.

2. Playing

  • Your first 3 plays are free. After that, plays are sold as credits: 25¢ for one play or $1 for a four-play pack. A $5 lifetime pass gives unlimited plays.
  • Free plays are consumed before paid credits. Lifetime-pass holders are not debited.
  • Runs you complete are submitted to the public leaderboard under the player name you enter. Do not enter a name that impersonates another person or contains another person's personal information.

The app never holds your funds and never holds your keys: signing happens in your wallet, and we will never ask for a seed phrase or private key, anywhere.

4. The Jupiter swap surface

"Stack real ZEC" opens Jupiter's official plugin to swap SOL for OmniBridge bridged ZEC on Solana. That swap is between you, your wallet, and Jupiter under Jupiter's terms; Zert Goblin never holds funds in it and is not a party to the swap. A 0.5% referral fee is wired into that surface but is currently inactive and charges nothing; if it is ever activated, the in-app disclosure will say so before you swap.
